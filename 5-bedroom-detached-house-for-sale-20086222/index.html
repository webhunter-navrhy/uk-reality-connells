Description

SUMMARY

A well-appointed, established and private home boasting five bedrooms, three bathroom's including two en-suites, double garage, large driveway in a quiet cul-de-sac location south of Hereford City. With front and rear gardens, this home is ideal for families and those looking to upsize.

DESCRIPTION

Upon approach, buyer's are greeted with a large driveway suitable for numerous vehicles and a double garage to the right. Gated access from the driveway welcomes the front garden. With blooming plants and a private patio area, this creates a warm welcome for those arriving. A central doorway with overhang gives access to the entrance hall, an open space with access to two living spaces, a staircase to the first floor and the downstairs cloakroom. To the left, a full length lounge with wood burner and doors to rear garden. To the right an open, dual aspect, kitchen diner with integrated appliances and access to a private seating area or third reception space, once more giving access to the private rear garden. With option to access the double garage for additional storage and boasting power and lighting, the rear garden offers a private patio area once more. The first floor comprises a landing, master bedroom with en-suite shower room, additional four bedrooms, with another en-suite and family bathroom also. Some bedrooms further benefit from fitted storage with the home itself offering double glazing and central heating throughout. Situated on the popular Saxon Gate estate. buyers can expect to be in close proximity to a number of local amenities including shops, schools, a public house and fish & chip shop whilst also having nearby children's play parks and countryside walks making this an ideal situation for a family.
